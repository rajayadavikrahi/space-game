HELP

The grid is down. Your lamp is the only light in the arena.

Energy orbs can only be picked up while the lamp is burning, and every orb you collect puts charge back into the battery. Enemies caught in the beam move faster, so the light that lets you see them is also what brings them down on you. Switch the lamp off to slow them down, but in the dark you are nearly blind.

When the battery runs out the lamp dies and only recharges while it is off.

Grab power-ups for special abilities:

  • Shield - Temporary invincibility
  • Speed - Move faster
  • 2X - Double score multiplier
  • Lantern - Refills the lamp
